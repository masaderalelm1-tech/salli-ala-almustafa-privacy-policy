Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Salli Ala Almustafa application handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

The application is designed to provide Islamic content and services related to sending prayers upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information from users unless it is explicitly required for a specific feature.

2. Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information. The application does not require users to create an account in order to use its main features.

3. Notifications

The application may send notifications and reminders according to the preferences selected by the user. Notification settings can be changed or disabled through the application or the device settings.

4. Audio and Video Content

The application may provide Islamic audio and video content. Some video content may be provided through external services such as YouTube. When external services are used, their own privacy policies and terms may apply.

5. Quran Content

The application may provide Quranic text and related content. The Quran content required for the application's core features may be stored locally on the device and does not require users to provide personal information.

6. Device Permissions

The application may request only the permissions necessary for its features. Permissions are used only for their intended functionality and are not used to collect unnecessary personal information.

7. Third-Party Services

Certain features may use third-party services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party service used through the application.

8. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application is intended to provide general Islamic educational and religious content.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with the updated version of the policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
